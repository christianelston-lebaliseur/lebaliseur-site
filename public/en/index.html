Your customers are searching for you. They're finding your competitors.

On Google, the top three take almost everything.

For local searches, Google shows a map with three businesses. Most customers stop there. The others exist, but nobody sees them.

And search is changing: more and more people simply ask an AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google's AI Mode). It names two or three businesses, picked from the same signals: listings, reviews, directories and a clear website.

What keeps you out is often simple: an incomplete Google profile, wrong opening hours, few recent reviews, or a website that's slow or hard to read on a phone.
