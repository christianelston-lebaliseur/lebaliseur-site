Privacy

Data controller

Chris Elston, Le Baliseur, chris@lebaliseur.fr.

Data collected

The audit request form collects your name, business name, email or phone number, website address, what you need and, if you took it, your online check score. This data is used only to answer your request.

The online visibility check collects your business name, town and activity, website address, first name, email and, if you give it, your phone number. To calculate the score it reads the public information on your Google profile and your website. The result and your details are kept so we can contact you about this check, never for a newsletter. To prevent abuse, an anonymised fingerprint of your IP address is kept to limit the number of checks per day.

Legal basis and retention

Processing is based on your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. Messages are kept while your request is handled and for at most three years after the last contact, then deleted.

Recipients

Your data is never sold or passed on. The online check and its results are hosted by Cloudflare; business information comes from Google (Places and PageSpeed). The form message is delivered by email through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), and nothing is stored on this site. The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To block bots, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that you're a person without advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are enabled, they use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to chris@lebaliseur.fr. You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
