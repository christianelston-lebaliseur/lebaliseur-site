Which option for which situation

Do it yourself

If you enjoy building, have the time and a small budget. Don't forget the Google profile, directories and upkeep: that's often where it stalls.

Online freelancer

If you have a specific need (a logo, one page) and know exactly what you want. Check who owns the domain and the accounts.

Traditional web agency

If you have a large project: an online shop with many products, an app, ad campaigns, several specialists to coordinate.

Le Baliseur

If you're a small business in Annecy or Haute-Savoie and want to be found on Google, on the map and in AI answers, with one person looking after it all.

When Le Baliseur isn't the right choice

  • An online shop with a large catalogue, or a bespoke app
  • Running paid ad campaigns (Google Ads, Meta)
  • A large organisation that needs a team of several people working in parallel
  • Private individuals: I only work with businesses and independents

If that's you, I'll say so at the audit stage, and point you elsewhere if I can.

Common questions

Web agency or freelancer for a website in Annecy?

An agency brings a team and a process; a freelancer brings flexibility and often a lower price. Le Baliseur is a one-person agency: a single point of contact, like a freelancer, with published prices and Google and AI support, like an agency.

Wix or a professional for a small business?

Wix or Squarespace work if you have time to do everything: website, Google profile, directories and upkeep. A professional saves you that time and avoids mistakes that cost customers: different details on different sites, an incomplete profile, a site that's slow on phones.

How much does a website from Le Baliseur cost?

A one-page site is €490 and a business website of up to 5 pages is €890, with the Google profile and 3 months of support included. Support starts at €49 a month with no commitment. All prices are on the Pricing page.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, website and accounts (Google, social media) are in your name. If you stop support, the site is transferred to an account in your name, free of charge.

Can you guarantee first place on Google or in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody can honestly promise it. I work on what Google and AI tools use to choose: a complete profile, consistent details everywhere, recent reviews and a clear website.
