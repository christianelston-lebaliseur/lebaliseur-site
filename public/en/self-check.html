How visible are you on Google?

A real check, in about a minute: we look at your Google profile, your map position against competitors, your website, its speed, how well AI assistants can read it and what they answer when people search for what you do. You get a score out of 100 and your three priorities.

What the check measures

Six areas, scored together out of 100. Each one matters for being found, then chosen.

  • Technical health: secure site, open to Google, working pages, mobile speed.
  • On-page SEO: titles, descriptions, content, and your town in the right places.
  • Google profile and local: profile found and complete, reviews, map position against competitors.
  • AI search readiness: structured data, common questions answered, AI crawlers allowed.
  • Recommended by AI: what an AI assistant answers when people search for your activity in your town.
  • Trust and contact: legal notice, tap-to-call, online booking.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes, with no obligation. You get your score and three priorities by email. I only contact you about this check.

What do you do with my details?

They're used only for this check: no newsletter, never sold, deleted on request. The privacy page has the details.

My score is low. Is that bad?

No. It mostly shows what's easiest to improve. Your three priorities are ranked by points gained for the effort.

What happens next?

If you like, I'll walk you through the full report for free: around thirty checks, your exact position against three competitors and a step-by-step plan.
