Starter pack

Your business visible from day one, with nothing to pay upfront.

When you're starting out, cash is tight and there are a thousand things to do. The starter pack gives you a website, a Google profile that's right and monthly support with nothing to pay upfront: one fixed monthly payment, so your budget goes into your business.

Get my free audit

The price

€0 upfront

Then €89 a month for 24 months, by direct debit, from the day the site goes live. Domain name in your name and paid by you (about €10–20 a year). By application, 3 places per quarter. VAT not applicable, art. 293 B of the French tax code.

What's included

  • A business website of up to 5 pages, fast and easy to read on a phone
  • The Google & AI visibility pack: Google profile, Bing, Apple Maps and directories
  • Essential monthly support: profile kept up to date, small website changes, a report every month
  • A launch meeting to sharpen your offer and your target customers
  • The website is yours at the end of the 24 months, or sooner if you settle the balance

Who it's for

  • Businesses that have just registered, or are about to
  • Based in Annecy or Haute-Savoie
  • With a clear offer and an idea of their customers
  • Ready to commit for 24 months

How it works

  1. ApplyTell me about your project in a few lines, using the audit form.
  2. Meet30 minutes at your place or a café: your offer, your customers, your goals. I'll tell you honestly whether the pack suits you.
  3. SignA simple contract and a direct debit mandate. Nothing is taken until the site goes live.
  4. LaunchGoogle profile in a week, website in about three weeks, then monthly support.

Common questions

Why is there a selection?

Because I'm committing two years alongside you and places are limited. The meeting checks that a website and Google are the right priority for you, and not money spent too early.

How much is it in total?

€2,136 over two years, everything included. That's only slightly more than buying the website and Essential support separately (€2,066), but with nothing upfront.

What if I stop before 24 months?

You can settle the remaining payments and keep the website. The contract sets everything out from the start.
