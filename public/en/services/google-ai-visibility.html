Common questions

Do I need a website to be on Google?

No. A well-kept Google profile is often enough to start. A website helps convince people and covers more detailed searches.

Can you guarantee first place?

No, and nobody can honestly promise it. I work on the signals Google and AI tools use: a complete profile, consistent details and recent reviews.
