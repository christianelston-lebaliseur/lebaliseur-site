Where to start?

Three situations come up again and again. The free audit confirms which one is yours.

  • People can't find you on Google. Start with the Google profile and AI visibility (€290): it is what brings calls and direction requests from local searches. The website comes next, if you really need one.
  • People find you, but your website doesn't convince them. The business website (from €890, Google profile included) is built to turn visits into calls and bookings, on phones first.
  • You're starting a business. The starter pack brings together a website, Google profile and support with nothing upfront: €89 a month over 24 months, by application.

After that, monthly support (from €49 a month) keeps your profile up to date, helps you answer reviews and measures results. Social media is added when it really matters for your business.

Common questions

Do I need every service?

No. The audit tells you honestly what will bring results fastest. Many businesses start with the Google profile alone, and only add a website if it helps.

How long before I see results?

A complete Google profile often makes a difference within a few weeks. Map rankings usually take two to four months to improve clearly.

Do you work outside Annecy?

Yes. I travel across Haute-Savoie to meet you, and I also work remotely with businesses across Europe and the UK, by video call and phone, in English or French.
