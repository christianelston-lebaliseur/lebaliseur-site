Terms of service

In force from 1 December 2026. The French version, Conditions générales de prestation, is the legally binding one.

1. Scope

These terms apply to all Le Baliseur services: website design and redesign, visibility on Google and in AI tools, social media, monthly and yearly support, and the starter pack. These services are for businesses only: companies, shops, tradespeople and the self-employed. An accepted quote and any specific terms in it take precedence over these terms.

2. Provider

3. Quotes and orders

Every service starts with a free quote, valid for 30 days. An order is confirmed when the quote is accepted in writing (signature or email) and, for websites and one-off services, when the deposit is paid.

4. Prices

Prices are those on the Pricing page on the date of the quote, in euros. VAT not applicable, art. 293 B of the French tax code. The price on an accepted quote is fixed.

5. Payment

Websites and one-off services: 50% on order, 50% on delivery. Monthly support: each month, in advance. Yearly support: once, in advance. Invoices are payable by bank transfer or direct debit within 15 days of their date. No discount is given for early payment.

Late payment incurs penalties at three times the French statutory interest rate, payable without reminder, and a fixed €40 recovery fee (art. L441-10 and D441-5 of the French Commercial Code). If payment is more than 30 days late, ongoing work may be paused after a reminder.

6. Timing and cooperation

Lead times (1 to 3 weeks for a website, depending on the project) start once the necessary content is received: text, photos, account access. The client agrees to provide these and to reply to proposals within a reasonable time; any delay on their side moves delivery back accordingly.

7. Feedback and changes during the build

While a website is being built, the client's feedback and changes of direction are included, within the scope of the quote (number of pages and planned features). Extra pages or features are quoted separately.

8. Support plans

An update session means up to one hour of work, requested by email and done within five working days. Unused sessions can be carried over within the same quarter, then lapse. The Visibility and Growth plans have a three-month minimum, then continue month to month; they can be cancelled with one month's notice by email. The Essential plan has no commitment. The yearly plan runs for twelve months.

Every active plan includes one rebrand per twelve-month period: applying a new logo, colours, fonts and photos to the existing site, Google profile and social media, and updating the text on existing pages. Logo design, a complete rebuild, new pages or new features are quoted separately.

9. Hosting and domain name

Website hosting is included while a support plan is active. The domain name is registered in the client's name, and the client pays for its renewal. When support ends, the website is transferred to an account in the client's name, free of charge.

10. Ownership

Once paid in full, the website, the content created for the client and the accounts opened in their name (Google, social media, hosting) belong to the client. The client confirms they hold the rights to the text, photos and logos they supply. Le Baliseur may show the work as a reference unless the client objects in writing.

11. Launch price and starter pack

The launch price (30% off the first one-off service) is reserved for the first ten clients, in exchange for an honest testimonial and permission to publish their results. The starter pack is granted by application, for 24 months; its early-exit terms are set out in the pack contract. The two offers cannot be combined.

12. Liability

Le Baliseur has a best-efforts obligation. Rankings on Google and mentions by AI tools depend on third-party services: no position can be guaranteed. Le Baliseur is not liable for outages or changes to those third-party services.

See the Privacy page.

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by French law. Any dispute falls under the courts of Annecy, after an attempt at amicable settlement.
