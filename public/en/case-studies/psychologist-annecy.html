Case study · Psychology practice, Annecy

A reassuring website, and the right address everywhere at last

Caroline Lillaz is a clinical psychologist in Annecy. Her old website was hard to read on a phone and still showed an old address. I rebuilt the site and brought her Google profile and directory listings back into line.

Client
Caroline Lillaz, psychologist
Launched

Services

See the live site (in French)

Before and after : on a phone

Most patients discover a practice on their phone. That is where the difference matters most.

What changed

The problem

The contact page still showed an old address, different from the practice's.

What I did

One address, 12 avenue Gambetta, on the website, the Google profile and the directories.

The problem

Pale grey text on a grey background, hard to read, and no clear button to book an appointment.

What I did

A calm, readable style, and a Book an appointment button on every page that starts the call.

The problem

Every page had the same long title, so Google didn't know what to show for each search.

What I did

One page per reason for consulting (addictions, anxiety, burnout, perinatal support…), each with its own title, and the old addresses redirected to keep the search rankings.

Before and after : on a computer

The results

The site has been live since November 2026. Results take two to three months to show : visibility on Google, clicks to the website and calls from the Google profile. I will publish them here, with the real numbers.
